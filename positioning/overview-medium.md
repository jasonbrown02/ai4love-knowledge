# AI4Love Overview

The intelligence layer for nonprofit teams.

---

## What AI4Love is

AI4Love connects to the systems your team already uses and surfaces the relationship patterns those systems collectively show.

It runs above your stack. Not inside it.

Your CRM stays your CRM. Your email platform stays your email platform. Your donation processor, your volunteer system, your events tool — all unchanged. AI4Love reads activity across them, finds the patterns no single tool can see, and tells your staff who needs attention this week.

That's the whole thing.

---

## What we believe

Five principles. They came before the product. The product is how we live them.

**1. Relationship signals already exist. Fragmentation hides them.**

Every system your team uses already captures signals about supporter relationships. Donation history. Volunteer hours. Event attendance. Email engagement. The signals exist. They sit in separate systems that do not talk to each other. The work is connecting what you already have, not collecting more.

**2. Detect patterns deterministically. Generate narrative selectively.**

A pattern is either present or it is not. Pattern detection should be rule-based and auditable. AI is for translating detected patterns into language a human can act on. Not for deciding which supporters matter.

**3. Silence beats incorrect output.**

When confidence is low, say nothing. A missed insight costs less than a wrong insight presented as fact. Suppression is a feature.

**4. Humans remain responsible for outreach.**

No supporter should ever receive a message a human did not write and approve. Restraint protects institutional trust. Automation belongs in internal workflows. Never in donor-facing communication.

**5. Relationships, not conversions.**

Supporters are people in relationship with your organization. Not conversion targets. Optimize for relationship continuity, not transaction frequency. The work compounds.

## What we refuse to do

Three refusals. Each one defines what AI4Love will never do, no matter how the market evolves.

**We do not write back to your systems.**

Our insights stay in our dashboard. Your CRM stays clean. Your staff decides what, if anything, goes back into your system of record.

**We do not launch campaigns.**

We surface the twenty people who need attention this week. Your team decides who reaches out and what they say.

**We do not send anything to your supporters.**

Every word that reaches a donor or volunteer is written by a human on your team. We never draft. We never send.

## How it works

AI4Love is hub-and-spoke architecture, inverted.

Your existing systems are the spokes. They keep doing what they already do. We connect to them through secure OAuth — your credentials, your control, revocable at any time.

A unified activity model brings the signals together. Every donation, volunteer shift, event attendance, and email engagement maps to one shared structure. Not in your CRM. In a normalized layer your team can read but never has to maintain.

Ten agents run nightly. Each one watches for a specific pattern. Who is drifting away. Who is ready for deeper engagement. Who deserves recognition. Who needs a human touch. Who paused their recurring gift and might respond to a quiet call. The patterns are detected by rules, not guessed by AI. The AI only writes the explanation, in language your team can act on.

Two sources of context shape what your team sees.

**KindMind** is our ongoing brain of nonprofit industry best practices. A growing library of sector research, proven approaches, and patterns observed across foundations. When an agent surfaces a recommendation, KindMind grounds it in what already works in the nonprofit world. Not generic AI guesses. Sector practice.

**ClientVault** is your organization's own knowledge, gathered in one place. Past campaigns, donor history, mission framing, team context. When an agent surfaces a recommendation, ClientVault pulls in what your organization already knows so every suggestion reflects your voice, your priorities, and your history.

Together they make every insight specific to your organization. Sector wisdom on one side. Your wisdom on the other.

Your dashboard shows you what the agents found. You decide what to do.

That's it.

## What your team sees

Four pages. Each answers one question.

**Pulse.** What is happening now? Real-time momentum across donors, volunteers, and engaged supporters.

**Insights.** What patterns matter? Aggregated findings across the ten agents, including cross-agent alerts.

**Nurture.** How healthy are supporter groups? Segment health spectrum: Thriving, Steady, Cooling, At-Risk, Lapsed.

**Connect.** Who needs a personal touch? Individual-level recommendations, organized as Celebrate, Reconnect, or Appreciate.

Every recommendation includes the context behind it. Your staff sees why a supporter surfaced, what the data shows, and what kind of outreach might fit. Then your staff decides.

## What "1% Can" means

Small acts of care, done consistently, change the world.

A timely thank-you. A handwritten note when it matters. A check-in when a long-time supporter goes quiet. None of these scale through automation. All of them compound when staff have time to notice the right moments.

AI4Love gives your team that time back. Not by sending the thank-you. By making sure your team sees the moment that deserves one.

1% more attention. 1% more care. 1% more connection. Repeated across thousands of moments. That is how movements form.
