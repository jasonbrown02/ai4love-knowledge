## What AI4Love is

AI4Love connects to the systems your team already uses and surfaces the relationship patterns those systems collectively show.

It runs above your stack. Not inside it.

Your CRM stays your CRM. Your email platform stays your email platform. Your donation processor, your volunteer system, your events tool, all unchanged. AI4Love reads activity across them, finds the patterns no single tool can see, and tells your staff who needs attention this week.

That's the whole thing.

## What we believe

Five principles. They came before the product. The product is how we live them.

**1. Relationship signals already exist. Fragmentation hides them.**

Every system your team uses already captures signals about supporter relationships. Donation history. Volunteer hours. Event attendance. Email engagement. The signals exist. They sit in separate systems that do not talk to each other.

The work is connecting what you already have, not collecting more. Most nonprofit teams already have ten times more data than they have time to read. Adding another database does not solve the problem. Connecting the existing ones does.

**2. Detect patterns deterministically. Generate narrative selectively.**

A pattern is either present or it is not. Pattern detection should be rule-based and auditable. If a supporter has not given in 18 months but attended two recent events, that is a fact. If their email engagement dropped 60% over the same period, that is also a fact. The pattern is the math.

AI is for translating detected patterns into language a human can act on. Not for deciding which supporters matter. Not for inventing facts. Not for guessing motivation.

**3. Silence beats incorrect output.**

When confidence is low, say nothing. A missed insight costs less than a wrong insight presented as fact. A vendor who guesses to fill the dashboard erodes trust the moment the staff catches the error. A vendor who stays silent when the data is thin earns trust over time.

Suppression is a feature. Not a limitation.

**4. Humans remain responsible for outreach.**

No supporter should ever receive a message a human did not write and approve. Restraint protects institutional trust. Automation belongs in internal workflows. Never in donor-facing communication.

The moment an AI vendor sends an email in your voice, your relationship with that supporter is mediated by software. That is not the work nonprofits are built to do. AI4Love stops at the edge of your team. Your staff carries the message the rest of the way.

**5. Relationships, not conversions.**

Supporters are people in relationship with your organization. Not conversion targets. Optimize for relationship continuity, not transaction frequency. The work compounds.

A donor who feels seen for ten years is worth more than a donor who is funneled through three campaigns and lapses. AI4Love measures recency, not just amount. It values the volunteer with no donations as much as the major gift donor with no other engagement. Both are part of your community.

## What we refuse to do

Three refusals. Each one defines what AI4Love will never do, no matter how the market evolves.

**We do not write back to your systems.**

Our insights stay in our dashboard. Your CRM stays clean. Your staff decides what, if anything, goes back into your system of record. No surprise fields. No AI-generated tags. No vendor-owned data living in your tools.

**We do not launch campaigns.**

We surface the twenty people who need attention this week. Your team decides who reaches out and what they say. No automated appeals. No sequences triggered by the platform. No campaign decisions made by software.

**We do not send anything to your supporters.**

Every word that reaches a donor or volunteer is written by a human on your team. We never draft. We never send.

## How it works

AI4Love is hub-and-spoke architecture, inverted.

Your existing systems are the spokes. They keep doing what they already do. We connect to them through secure OAuth. Your credentials. Your control. Revocable at any time.

A unified activity model brings the signals together. Every donation, volunteer shift, event attendance, and email engagement maps to one shared structure. Not in your CRM. In a normalized layer your team can read but never has to maintain.

Ten agents run nightly. Each one watches for a specific pattern. Who is drifting away. Who is ready for deeper engagement. Who deserves recognition. Who needs a human touch. Who paused their recurring gift and might respond to a quiet call. The patterns are detected by rules, not guessed by AI. The AI only writes the explanation, in language your team can act on.

Two sources of context shape what your team sees.

**KindMind** is our ongoing brain of nonprofit industry best practices. A growing library of sector research, proven approaches, and patterns observed across foundations. When an agent surfaces a recommendation, KindMind grounds it in what already works in the nonprofit world. Not generic AI guesses. Sector practice.

**ClientVault** is your organization's own knowledge, gathered in one place. Past campaigns, donor history, mission framing, team context. When an agent surfaces a recommendation, ClientVault pulls in what your organization already knows so every suggestion reflects your voice, your priorities, and your history.

Together they make every insight specific to your organization. Sector wisdom on one side. Your wisdom on the other.

Your dashboard shows you what the agents found. You decide what to do.

That's it.

## The ten agents

Each agent answers one question. They run on staggered schedules every night.

**Agent 1: At-Risk Detection.** Who is drifting away?

**Agent 2: Conversion Opportunities.** Who is ready for more?

**Agent 3: Recognition Triggers.** Who deserves celebration?

**Agent 4: Campaign Intelligence.** What individual patterns matter?

**Agent 5: Relationship Deepening.** Who needs a human touch with no ask attached?

**Agent 6: Cross-Agent Intelligence.** What does multi-agent overlap reveal?

**Agent 7: KindMind Enrichment.** What does sector research recommend?

**Agent 8: Queue Cleanup.** Did anyone get missed?

**Agent 9: Lapsed Donor Resurrection.** Who has been silent for five-plus years but should be called?

**Agent 9b: Recurring Pause Resurrection.** Whose monthly giving paused, and is it worth a personal nudge?

Agents 1 through 7 are intelligence agents. They surface relationship patterns. Agent 8 is operational housekeeping. Agents 9 and 9b focus on the deeply lapsed, where the highest-leverage relationships often live.

Every agent applies the same rule: detect deterministically, narrate selectively, surface only when confidence justifies attention.

## What your team sees

Four pages. Each answers one question.

**Pulse.** What is happening now?

Real-time momentum across donors, volunteers, and engaged supporters. New gifts. Recent event sign-ups. Email engagement spikes. The pulse of your community in one view. No AI interpretation. Just the data, organized so you can see today's reality at a glance.

**Insights.** What patterns matter?

Aggregated findings across the ten agents. At-risk supporters ranked by urgency and lifetime value. Conversion candidates ready for the next conversation. Recognition triggers waiting to be acted on. Cross-agent alerts when the same supporter shows up in multiple analyses. This is where your team starts most mornings.

**Nurture.** How healthy are supporter groups?

Segment-level dashboard showing the health of your donor base, your volunteer base, and your engaged-but-undeclared supporters. Each group sits on a spectrum: Thriving, Steady, Cooling, At-Risk, Lapsed. You see fatigue before it becomes lapse. You see strength before it becomes complacency.

**Connect.** Who needs a personal touch?

Individual-level recommendations, organized as Celebrate, Reconnect, or Appreciate. The supporter who just hit a giving milestone. The volunteer ready to become a donor. The quiet champion who deserves gratitude with no ask attached. Every recommendation includes the context behind it: why this supporter surfaced, what the data shows, what kind of outreach might fit.

Your staff sees the moment. Your staff decides whether and how to act.

## The supporter health spectrum

Status is derived from activity, not from manual entry. Every supporter sits in one of five bands based on days since last activity across donations, volunteer shifts, event attendance, and engagement.

**Active.** Under 90 days since last activity. Currently engaged.

**Steady.** 91 to 180 days. Slowing but still recent.

**Cooling.** 181 to 365 days. Within a year but quieting.

**At-Risk.** 366 to 730 days. Drifting. Primary focus of at-risk detection.

**Lapsed.** Over 730 days. Long-gone. Primary focus of resurrection agents, filtered for lifetime value.

The bands are not labels. They are the result of math, recalculated every day. A supporter who hasn't donated in two years but volunteered last month is Active. A supporter who gave $100,000 a decade ago and never returned is Lapsed. The spectrum reflects what is actually happening now.

## Bounded custody

Your systems of record stay your systems of record.

AI4Love never writes back to your CRM, email platform, or enrichment source. Connections are read-only and run through OAuth, gateway-managed by Nango. Your credentials. Your scopes. Your revocation rights. If you disconnect us tomorrow, our access ends the same day.

What AI4Love holds is one bounded working base per organization, in AI4Love's Airtable account, isolated from every other organization's, containing only the normalized activity signals and the insights the nightly agents need. It exists while you are a customer plus a 90-day exit window, then it is deleted. A full export or immediate deletion is available on request at any time.

Owned Copy is planned: a nightly, write-only sync of your people, activity and insight records to a file your organization owns in its own workspace. It is designed and available to be provisioned per organization and is not yet running in production. We describe it in the present tense only once it is.

For procurement teams: this answers four questions at once. Does the vendor write into our systems of record? No. Does the vendor keep our data after we leave? No, deleted after the 90-day exit window. Does the vendor force centralization? No. Can humans override every recommendation? Yes.

Bounded custody is not a feature toggle. It is the architecture, and it is documented in the public Trust Center.

## Who AI4Love is built for

Foundations and nonprofit organizations between 1,000 and 15,000 active supporters. Teams of four to twenty people who already have the tools they need but lack the time to see what those tools collectively show.

Most of our work happens in two sectors.

**Hospital foundations.** Where institutional trust is the asset. Where a single embarrassing AI insight can damage a relationship with a board member or a major donor. Where the staff is small, the campaign cycle is long, and the cost of a missed stewardship moment is measured in years.

**University and college advancement.** Where decentralization is the operating reality. Where central advancement, faculty-based development, alumni relations, and athletics each see their own slice of the donor. Where the data definition fights are constant and the political risk of a wrong recommendation is high. AI4Love coordinates without forcing any unit to change workflow.

Both sectors share the same procurement reality. Governance matters. Privacy reviews are real. The wrong AI tool creates more risk than value. AI4Love is built for the buyer who already knows this.
