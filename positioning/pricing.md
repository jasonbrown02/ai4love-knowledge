# Pricing Structure

**Status:** Working draft, based on JBHF Pilot Proposal v2 (April 2026) plus post-meeting refinements (May 2026). Not yet validated against signed clients. Subject to change once first client completes 60-day pilot.

## Active supporter definition

Anyone with at least one recorded participation in the past 24 months. A donation, a volunteer shift, an event attendance, a campaign response. A database of 17,000 total records typically represents 10,000 to 12,000 active supporters. Clients are not billed for dormant history.

## The lapsed pool

Everything older than 24 months is the lapsed pool. Not part of billing, but not invisible either. Agents 9 (Lapsed Donor Resurrection) and 10 (Recurring Pause Resurrection) run nightly across the full ten-year historical backfill, surfacing revivable relationships.

This is a meaningful product distinction. Most CRM platforms charge by total records. Foundations using those tools pay to keep history they cannot afford to mine manually. AI4Love charges only for active relationships and mines the history alongside them.

## Pilot pricing

- **Duration:** 60 days
- **Cost:** $2,000 CAD flat, paid upfront
- **Setup fee:** Permanently waived for pilot participants
- **Mutual exit clause:** Either party may end at day 60 with no further obligation
- **Includes:** Ten-year historical backfill (standard for all clients), weekly check-ins with Jason and Scott

## Backfill scope

- **Standard:** 10 years of historical data, backfilled during setup
- **Why standard:** Agents 9 and 10 need historical depth to surface lapsed donor candidates. Sector reality per Dwayne (JBHF): every foundation has 10+ years of donor history available.
- **Completion time:** 48 to 72 hours for foundations with under 10,000 active supporters. 72 to 96 hours for larger university/college advancement operations.
- **Cost to client:** Included in pilot fee. No separate backfill line item.

## Post-pilot subscription tiers (CAD, monthly)

| Tier | Active Supporters | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Up to 10,000 | $1,400 |
| Tier 2 | Up to 20,000 | $2,200 |
| Tier 3 | 20,000+ | Custom |

Active supporters only. Lapsed pool is mined automatically and included at no additional cost.

## Terms

- Month-to-month. No annual contract required.
- Unlimited users. No seat cap.
- No overage charges. No per-insight pricing. No charge for lapsed-pool mining.
- **Tier protection:** If active supporter count grows during the first 6 months of subscription, client remains at their starting tier for that period.

## What clients get

- Ten intelligence agents running nightly across connected platforms (1-8 against active supporters, 9-10 against the full ten-year lapsed pool)
- Four dashboard views: Pulse, Insights, Nurture, Connect
- Direct Claude connection (MCP) for ad-hoc questions in plain language
- KindMind sector intelligence enrichment
- ClientVault organizational context layer

## Margin reality check

Cross-reference against [cost-model.md](cost-model.md):

- COGS at 5,000 supporters: ~$405 to $440/month (System Spec v3.2 estimate)
- Tier 1 price: $1,400 CAD/month (~$1,030 USD)
- Estimated Tier 1 gross margin: ~57-60%

**This is below the 77% margin target from System Spec v3.2.** Two reads:
1. Revisit Tier 1 pricing upward once first pilot validates value
2. Accept lower margin on Tier 1 as the cost of market entry, recovering margin at Tier 2 and Tier 3

**Note on 10-year backfill cost:** Original backfill estimate was ~$420 USD for 6,000 supporters across 2 years of data. Ten years of data could increase this 3-5x depending on data density. Verify against actual JBHF backfill before next prospect to confirm pilot fee covers the cost.
