# Positioning

The locked three-line hierarchy from Pass A v3. Each line does a different job. They do not compete with each other.

## The three lines

### Line 1: Master category

> The intelligence layer for nonprofit teams.

**Job:** reduces confusion. Tells a stranger what we are in one sentence.

**Source:** appears verbatim in the System Spec, in Scott's HHS demo, and in architecture documentation. Discovered, not invented.

**Surfaces:**
- AI4Love.ca homepage hero (primary)
- Scott's email signature
- Scott's LinkedIn headline
- Conference one-pager top of page
- First sentence of any cold outreach
- Investor deck slide 1
- Demo deck slide 1

### Line 2: Strategic inversion

> AI4Love coordinates decentralization. Most vendors force centralization.

**Job:** creates strategic curiosity. Names the architectural contrast against every CRM hub.

**Source:** derived from the Bounded Retention model in System Spec v3.5 section 1.

AI4Love holds one working Airtable base per organization in AI4Love's account as a service provider. The base is isolated per organization. It exists only while service is active plus a 90-day exit window, then it is deleted. Full export is available on request. The client organization remains the institution responsible for the personal information under FIPPA. An organization-held mirror (Owned Copy) is designed but not running in production and is described only as planned.

**Surfaces:**
- AI4Love.ca below-the-fold positioning paragraph
- LinkedIn thought leadership posts (the conceptual hook)
- Conference keynote opener
- Investor pitch architecture slide
- Sales follow-up email when discovery calls confirm a buyer is comparing us to a hub-model competitor

### Line 3: Behavioral doctrine

> We surface. We never send.

**Job:** builds trust. Tells the buyer what we refuse to do.

**Source:** HHS demo room, where it landed without needing explanation.

**Surfaces:**
- AI4Love.ca on the refusals or trust section
- Demo slide 2 or 3, right after the headline
- Discovery call opening: "Before I show you what AI4Love does, let me tell you what it refuses to do."
- Procurement-response template (RFP answers)
- Conference Q&A when asked about AI safety or supporter trust

## Live conversation metaphor (demo-only)

The raindrops story: "We collect every raindrop of activity across your systems."

Use in live demos and conference talks where you can hand-gesture and build the picture. Do not use as static homepage copy. Metaphors work better in motion than on paper.

## What the category does not claim

- Not a CRM
- Not a replacement for fundraising software
- Not enterprise SaaS
- Not trying to be Blackbaud, Bloomerang, Virtuous, Kindsight, or DonorPerfect

## What the category does claim

- We connect the systems nonprofit teams already use
- We surface what those systems collectively show
- We let the staff stay in the relationship
- We do this for a small number of organizations, completely
