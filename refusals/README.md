# The Three Refusals

The locked refusals from Pass A v3 Decision 3. These back the abstract restraint claim with specific actions every Tier 1 and Tier 2 competitor will perform.

## Why these three

Hospital foundations and university advancement offices are governance-heavy buyers. Their procurement process asks four institutional-risk questions:

1. What data does the vendor retain, and who remains responsible for it?
2. Does the vendor take autonomous action on our donors?
3. Does the vendor write into our systems of record?
4. Is the AI black-box or auditable?

The three refusals answer these questions on the side that reduces institutional risk.

---

## Refusal 1: We do not write back to your systems.

**Covers:** data-integrity and system-of-record risk.

**Competitor contrast:** Blackbaud writes NXT Ratings and Prospect Insights directly into RE NXT records. Bloomerang Penny creates segments inside Bloomerang. AI4Love surfaces insights to your team. Your staff decides what, if anything, goes back into the CRM.

## Refusal 2: We do not launch campaigns.

**Covers:** autonomous-action risk.

**Competitor contrast:** Bloomerang Penny launches campaigns. AI4Love surfaces the 20 people who need attention this week. Your team decides who reaches out and what they say.

## Refusal 3: We do not send anything to your supporters.

**Covers:** brand-voice and donor-trust risk.

**Competitor contrast:** Bloomerang, Virtuous, Blackbaud, Kindsight, and DonorPerfect all draft donor emails, thank-you letters, social posts. Most can send them. AI4Love does neither. Every word that reaches a supporter is written and sent by a human on your team.

---

## How to use these in sales conversations

**Discovery call opener:**
> "Before I show you what AI4Love does, let me tell you what it refuses to do."

**RFP answers:**
Drop the three refusals verbatim into the section answering vendor risk questions.

**Demo:**
Slide 2 or 3, right after the headline.

## Other refusals considered but not locked

Available if a sales conversation needs them. See the Pass A v3 Google Doc Decision 3 candidate list for the full eight.

- Refusal 4: We do not consolidate your data into a new system of record.
- Refusal 5: We do not screen your supporters for wealth.
- Refusal 6: We do not optimize donor experience for conversion.
- Refusal 7: We do not automate stewardship.
- Refusal 8: We do not bolt new AI features onto your existing stack every quarter.
