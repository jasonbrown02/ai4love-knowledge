# Marc-André Beaulieu — Director of Advancement Services, large Canadian university

**Archetype:** Trust-Layer Technical Buyer
**Motion:** A
**Tier:** Enterprise
**Engine:** Pulse + Insights

## Identity

40-48 years old. 6 years in current role, 16 years sector. Large Canadian research university in Quebec. Hybrid advancement model. 180,000 alumni records, 65,000 active engagement records, multiple faculties with embedded development staff. Career: gift processing → CRM analyst → led Salesforce transition after years in RE NXT. BCom plus institutional privacy training.

## A day in the life

Tuesday 7:52am. Opens Teams first. Central IT replied to last week's privacy questionnaire with 14 new questions. Before coffee, he is already explaining why Advancement cannot let every faculty run its own email list outside the approved stack.

Opens Salesforce, then data quality dashboard. Duplicate rate better than last quarter, but Medicine imported event attendees with inconsistent faculty codes again. Engineering wants a new microsite tool. Alumni Relations wants more self-serve engagement dashboards. VP wants one clean campaign view by Friday.

Proud that gift processing now closes batches by noon, not 4pm. Quietly prevented three bad imports this month. Has not gotten to documentation. Has not updated data dictionary.

Frustration: everyone wants analytics, no one wants standards. Institution says it wants innovation, then asks whether every new tool creates a privacy impact issue.

On his mind: "If this touches donor data, I own the risk, even when someone else bought the tool."

## Top 3 frustrations

1. Decentralized teams want freedom, central services owns the cleanup
2. Privacy reviews slow useful work, shadow systems spread faster than governance
3. Every dashboard request becomes a data definition fight
