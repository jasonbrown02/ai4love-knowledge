# Sarah McNeil — VP Philanthropy, Canadian hospital foundation

**Archetype:** Strategic Sponsor
**Motion:** A
**Tier:** Growth
**Engine:** Insights + Connect

## Identity

45-52 years old. 3 years in current role, 18 years sector experience. Regional hospital foundation in Ontario. $18M annual revenue, 22,000 donor records, 8,500 active supporters. Preparing a $75M diagnostic imaging and cancer care campaign. Career arc: annual giving → major gifts → campaign leadership. BA communications, CAGP and AHP training.

## A day in the life

Tuesday 8:14am. Opens Outlook first. Three emails from hospital CEO's office, two from board members, one from Director of Major Gifts asking which prospects to move into campaign cabinet pipeline. Opens RE NXT, then Excel pipeline file her team trusts more than the dashboard. Numbers don't match. Board report says campaign pipeline is strong. Her gut says it's thin after the top 40 names.

Frustration is not lack of data. Every answer arrives from a different person, system, and spreadsheet. Annual giving has one view of lapsed monthly donors. Major gifts has another view of capacity. Events has supporters who showed up twice this year but never made it into a portfolio.

Proud of a seven-figure verbal pledge last week. Less proud that two fundraisers look exhausted, and one high performer stopped volunteering for new prospects.

On her mind: "If we miss this campaign ramp, the hospital notices. If we push too hard, the team breaks."

## Top 3 frustrations

1. Pipeline confidence without pipeline truth
2. Staff burnout masked as "high performance"
3. Donor fatigue anxiety
