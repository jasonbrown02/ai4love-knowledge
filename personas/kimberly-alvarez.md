# Kimberly Alvarez — Director of Annual Giving, US health system foundation

**Archetype:** Operational Champion
**Motion:** A
**Tier:** Growth
**Engine:** Nurture + Insights

## Identity

36-44 years old. 2 years current role, 12 years sector. Multi-hospital US health system foundation in the Midwest. 90,000 constituent records across three legacy databases. Consolidating annual giving, grateful patient, events, and regional foundation brands. Career: direct response at children's hospital → digital annual giving → system foundation after merger. BA marketing, CASE and AHP training.

## A day in the life

Tuesday 8:03am. Opens the campaign calendar first. Not the CRM. The calendar tells her what is about to collide.

One regional hospital wants a service-line appeal. System marketing has heart health email scheduled Thursday. A former standalone foundation wants to send its legacy newsletter. Grateful patient has a new physician referral flow but no clean way to tell who already received three touches this month.

Opens Salesforce, then Classy, then Marketing Cloud. None answers her real question: "Who is tired of us, and who is ready for more?"

Proud of last month's digital acquisition results. Cost per donor below forecast, monthly conversion improved. Knows the numbers hide a problem. New donors enter one journey, event donors enter another, former regional donors still get language from the old brand. Has 4,800 lapsed donors but no useful way to rank them beyond last gift and amount.

On her mind: consolidation was sold as efficiency. So far it feels like every team kept its old habits and gained one more approval layer.

## Top 3 frustrations

1. Brand consolidation without journey consolidation
2. Annual donors treated as campaign outputs, not future relationships
3. Attribution fog across Salesforce, Classy, email, and regional event tools
