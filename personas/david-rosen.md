# David Rosen — AVP Development, US R1 research university

**Archetype:** Strategic Sponsor
**Motion:** A
**Tier:** Enterprise
**Engine:** Pulse + Insights + Nurture

## Identity

48-56 years old. 4 years current role, 22 years sector. Public R1 in the Midwest. $4B campaign. Federated advancement model with strong schools in Medicine, Engineering, Business, Athletics, Arts. 450,000 alumni and donor records. Career: school-based MGO → ED for Engineering → AVP overseeing university-wide development and campaign strategy. MBA, CASE leadership programs.

## A day in the life

Tuesday 7:36am. Opens his campaign dashboard first. Total looks good. Distribution does not.

Medicine is ahead. Engineering is behind but confident. Business has its own forecast. Athletics has donors drifting toward NIL collectives, and the central report barely reflects it. Provost wants interdisciplinary gifts. Deans want school credit. VP wants fewer surprises.

Opens CRM, then portfolio review deck from Prospect Development. Trusts the prospect team, but knows half the story lives outside the official moves. A donor attended an athletics dinner, gave to NIL, opened a scholarship impact email, and met with a dean. No one owns the whole picture.

Proud campaign has real momentum. Principal gift conversations happening. Deans engaged. Board leaning in.

On his mind: "The campaign will not fail because we lack ambition. It will fail if we keep letting each unit see only its slice."

## Top 3 frustrations

1. Decentralized schools create parallel truths about the same donors
2. Athletics and NIL blur donor intent, loyalty, and fatigue
3. Tech governance moves slower than campaign pressure

## What makes him say yes

- **Demo trigger:** AI4Love identifies cross-unit donor signals without forcing schools to give up their systems

## Buying authority

Signs alone under $25K USD for pilots in his portfolio. Co-approves $25K-$100K with Adv Services and IT. $100K+ requires VP/CFO/procurement. Blessing: VP Advancement, Adv Services, CIO/security, procurement, school EDs, sometimes athletics.

## Triggering events

- Organizational: campaign enters public phase, CRM migration or data warehouse planning, donor conflict across units becomes board-visible
- Personal: asked to explain weak pipeline coverage, dean challenges central data, athletics/NIL confusion reaches a major donor conversation

## Quote

> "Our problem is not lack of activity. It is that every unit sees activity as ownership."

## How AI4Love positioning lands

- **Master category:** strong. Federated model needs an intelligence layer, not another hub.
- **Strategic inversion:** kill-shot positive. "Coordinates decentralization" is exactly his architectural reality.
- **Behavioral doctrine:** strong. Removes the political risk that an automated tool would create with deans.

## How the three refusals land

1. **No write-back to systems:** strong. Respects each unit's CRM authority.
2. **No campaign launches:** strong. Removes the "central tool that overrides school strategy" fear.
3. **No sending to supporters:** strong. Eliminates risk of system mass-emailing a major donor inappropriately.
