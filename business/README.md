## Pricing snapshot

- **Core tier:** $1,800/mo target. Designed for orgs 1,000-5,000 active supporters.
- **Growth tier:** _to define_. Designed for orgs 5,000-10,000 active supporters.
- **Enterprise tier:** _to define_. Designed for orgs 10,000-15,000 active supporters and federated models.

## Active supporter definition for billing

Anyone with at least one recorded participation in the last 24 months. A donation, a volunteer shift, an event attendance, a campaign response. Avoids penalizing organizations for large historical databases.
